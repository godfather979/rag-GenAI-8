Job Advertisement: Adhoc/Temporary Position for Office Assistants /Clerks/Accountant
posted by administrator@spit.ac.in on April 27th, 2026 under
Notice
The Office of the SPIT invites applications for the adhoc/temporary position of
Office Assistant/Clerk/Accountant
.
Position:
Office Assistant / Clerk /Accountant
Key Responsibilities:
Handling office administration and documentation
Maintaining records and files systematically
Assisting in examination and academic office work
Data entry and report preparation
Required Skills & Qualifications:
Graduate in any discipline. For accountant B.Com.
Good proficiency in
MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
Strong organizational and communication skills
Basic knowledge of office procedures
Preferred:
Prior experience in academic or administrative offices
Selection Process:
Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview. Interviews are tentatively scheduled in the
third week of May
.
