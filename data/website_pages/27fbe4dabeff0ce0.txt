Invitation to Attend Workshop on Generative AI & Indian Knowledge System 30–31 Jan 2026
posted by administrator@spit.ac.in on January 8th, 2026 under
Announcements
,
Events
Greetings fellow Academicians / Students / Researchers / Professionals,
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering,
Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT), Mumbai
, is organizing a
two-day workshop
on
“Generative Artificial Intelligence Research Projects Development Inspired from Indian Knowledge System.”
This workshop aims to explore how concepts from the
Indian Knowledge System (IKS)
can contribute to modern
Generative AI research
, and why this integration is increasingly relevant in 2025 and beyond.
Workshop Details:
Dates:
30th – 31th January 2026
Venue:
Sardar Patel Institute of Technology, Andheri (W), Mumbai – 400058 or Online through Google meet
The sessions will be delivered by
eminent academicians and researchers
and are designed to benefit:
Students interested in AI and research
Faculty members
Researchers and academicians
Participants will gain insights into interdisciplinary research ideas, emerging AI trends, and practical directions for research project development.
Registration Link:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd_Me2ues6JGSuzoHLDABzNYLRPY8Qiw2bRq_TnoVQAKh6sBg/viewform
Seats are limited. Interested participants are encouraged to register at the earliest.
For any queries, feel free to contact us at +91 9619997797.
Regards,
