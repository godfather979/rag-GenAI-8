Alumni Meet, Pune 27th September, 2026
posted by administrator@spit.ac.in on September 30th, 2026 under
Events
On 27th September, alumni from 16+ graduation years, spanning 1999 to 2026, came together in Pune for an afternoon of reconnecting, reminiscing and networking.
There was laughter, old memories, new conversations, introductions over lunch, and plenty of reasons to stay connected.
But this is just the beginning. The purpose of these meets is simple. To bring our alumni together and help everyone have someone to reach out to.
To reconnect with old friends.
To reminisce about our SPIT days.
To build new connections.
To give back to Alma Mater
To Mentor a student
To open opportunities for Internships
To Bring CSR activities to the Institute
And to create opportunities to support and learn from one another.
