Applications are invited for ADHOC faculty in Master of Computer Application / Information Technology / Computer Engineering / Communication Skills
Eligibility Criteria for ADHOC Faculty in MCA/IT/Computer Engineering
Ph.D in Engineering with relevant UG and PG qualifications.
Candidates with M.E/M.Tech qualification from IITs/NITs/COEP/VJTI and pursuing PhD will also be considered.
Candidates with 1or 2 years of experience will be preferred.
Eligibility Criteria for ADHOC Faculty in “COMMUNICATION SKILLS”
UG and PG qualifications from the recognized institution/university.
Ph.D in the relevant faculty or pursuing PhD
Besides fulfilling the above qualifications, the candidate must have cleared the National Eligibility Test (NET) conducted by the UGC, CSIR or similar test accredited by the UGC like SLET/SET.
Candidates with 1or 2 years of experience will be preferred.
