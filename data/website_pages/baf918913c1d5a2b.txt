Cisco’s Todd Nightingale, writing in a
blog post
announcing the deal said that the kind of data that ThousandEyes provides around internet user experience is more important than ever as internet connections have come under tremendous pressure with huge numbers of employees working from home.
Incidentally two more students Murtaza Doctor (batch 2000) and Aniket Lawande (batch 2012) are also working with Thousandeyes.
