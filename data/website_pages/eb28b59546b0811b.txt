About
Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT) is a leading engineering institute located in the heart of Mumbai. As a constituent institution of
Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan
and an autonomous institute affiliated with the
University of Mumbai
, SPIT has established itself as one of India’s preferred destinations for engineering education through its commitment to academic excellence, innovation and value-based learning.
The institute is situated on a picturesque
40+ acre green campus
at Andheri (West), one of Mumbai’s well-connected educational hubs. The campus also houses four other prestigious institutions of Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan:
Bhavan’s College
Sardar Patel College of Engineering (SPCE)
S.P. Jain Institute of Management and Research (SPJIMR)
A.H. Wadia Higher Secondary School
Together, these institutions create a vibrant environment that promotes academic excellence and interdisciplinary learning.
Our History
In
1957
, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan conceived the idea of establishing an engineering college in Mumbai. This vision was realized with the inauguration of
Sardar Patel College of Engineering (SPCE)
on
19 August 1962
.
In
1995
, self-financed engineering programmes were introduced under the unaided wing of SPCE, offering undergraduate programmes in
Electronics Engineering
,
Computer Engineering
and
Information Technology
. A Master’s programme in Electronics Engineering was offered from 2005 to 2008. These programmes earned an excellent reputation in engineering education as well as in industry.
In
2005
, the unaided wing was established in its dedicated building under the name
Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan’s Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT)
. Since then, SPIT has continued to grow as a premier engineering institute while remaining affiliated with the
University of Mumbai
.
A Leading Engineering Institute
SPIT is recognized as one of the leading engineering institutes in Mumbai and has consistently strengthened its reputation through academic excellence, innovation and quality education. As an autonomous institute, SPIT enjoys the flexibility to continuously enhance its academic programmes while maintaining the highest standards of teaching and learning.
As a constituent institution of Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, SPIT strives to influence, practice and promote value-based growth. The institute pursues this mission through pedagogic innovations and pioneering academic programmes that have helped establish its distinctive identity in engineering education.
Over the years, SPIT has expanded its academic offerings by introducing postgraduate and doctoral programmes while continuing its commitment to excellence in engineering education. Today, the institute continues to prepare students to meet the challenges of an ever-evolving technological landscape through quality education, innovation and a strong foundation of values.
