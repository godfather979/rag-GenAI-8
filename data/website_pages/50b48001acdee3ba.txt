Rules & Regulations
In the noble profession of teaching, it is essential to have equally noble values and ethics. Such values are of importance to maintain the dignity of the profession and prevent it from being tainted by unethical practices. To ensure that, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan’s Sardar Patel Institute of Technology, prescribes the following code of conduct for its teaching faculty:
Impartiality
: To ensure quality and equal education to all.
Dignity
: To ensure the worthy of honor and respect from all stakeholders.
Integrity
: To inculcate the value of being honest and having strong moral principles.
Trust
: To develop a sense of reliability and confidence, mainly among students and other stakeholders.
Commitment
: To strive towards the organisational goals and missions with a sense of obligation.
Diligence
: To ensure careful and persistent work or effort towards the cause of education.
Respect
: To create a a sense of admiration elicited by the abilities, qualities and achievements of the institution.
